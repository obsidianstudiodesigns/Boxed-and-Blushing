Placing an order

  • Sending us an order on WhatsApp is a request for a quote. Your order is only accepted once we confirm it in writing and any payment we agree on is received.
  • Please check the spelling of every name, role and date you send us. We print exactly what you approve.
  • Give us as much time as you can. We will tell you if we cannot make your date.

Payment

Payment is made by the method and on the terms set out in your quote. We may ask for a deposit or full payment before we start on personalised items. We will never ask for your card details on WhatsApp.

Specials, such as our bridal party special, apply while they are advertised and to matching boxes in the same order. We confirm whether a special applies in your quote.

Delivery

We deliver across South Africa using a courier, at the cost in your quote. Delivery times are estimates from the courier. Please give us a full and correct address and a contact number, and tell us straight away if anything arrives damaged.

Safety

Some boxes contain candles, glassware, alcohol or small items. Keep candles away from children and never leave them burning unattended. Alcohol is only included in orders for adults over 18. Baby and children's items are chosen with care, but please check every item before use and keep small parts away from babies.
