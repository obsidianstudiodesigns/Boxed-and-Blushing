What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us when you order or ask a question, such as:

  • your name and cellphone number (WhatsApp shares your number with us when you message us)
  • the boxes and items you would like
  • the names and roles you want printed on gifts, which may include the names of other people, such as your bridal party or a child
  • your theme and colours, the date you need your order and your delivery town
  • your delivery address when we arrange courier delivery
  • anything else you include in your message

If you give us someone else's name to print on a gift, please only share what is needed for the gift and make sure they would be happy with it. For gifts for children, a parent or guardian should place the order.

How the order form works

This website has no online shop, no payment system and no database. When you build an order, the boxes you add are remembered only in your own browser (local storage) so they are still there if you reload the page. The "Send my order on WhatsApp" button opens WhatsApp on your device with your order already written out. Nothing is sent to us unless you press send in WhatsApp yourself.

Why we use it

  • to quote for, make, personalise and deliver your order
  • to answer your questions and keep you updated about your order
  • to keep the records the law requires

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we will not add you to any marketing list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to, for example with a courier company so they can deliver your order, or when the law requires it. WhatsApp and Facebook (Meta) carry our messages under their own privacy policies.

Photos of our work

We like to share photos of finished boxes on Facebook and on this website. Personalised items may show a first name. If you would rather we did not share a photo of your order, just tell us when you order, or ask us to remove a photo at any time.

This website, cookies and third parties

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. To load, your browser fetches files from these providers, which may record your IP address and browser details in their standard server logs:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces
  • jsDelivr, which supplies the icons

Links to WhatsApp and Facebook take you to services with their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep order messages only as long as we need them to make and deliver your order and handle any follow-up. Records of sales are kept for as long as tax law requires, usually five years, and then deleted.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, including keeping it on password-protected devices and accounts and only sharing it when needed.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • object to us using it
  • withdraw consent you have given

Contact us on the details above and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
