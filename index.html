    Good to know

    How do I order?

    Add the boxes you like to your order on this page, fill in the names and your colours, and send it to us on WhatsApp. You can also message or call us on 064 395 3029.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, we deliver across South Africa. Tell us your town or city and we confirm the delivery cost with your quote.

    Can I choose my own colours and items?

    Yes. Mix and match our products and we make the box up in your theme and colours. Send us a photo of your colour palette if you have one.

    What does a box cost?

    It depends on what goes into the box and how many you need. Our boxes are budget friendly, and we send you a quote on WhatsApp before anything is made.

    What can you personalise?

    Most items can carry a name and role: robes, slippers, tumblers, glasses, candles, notebooks, make-up bags and the box itself.

    How far ahead should I order?

    As early as you can, especially for weddings and bigger orders. Send us your date and we will tell you if we can make it in time.
